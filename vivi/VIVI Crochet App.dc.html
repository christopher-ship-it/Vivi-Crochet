ABOUT
SINCE 2024 · COIMBATORE

VIVI
CROCHET

One hook, a bag of cotton yarn, and a kitchen table in Coimbatore.

Vivi learnt to crochet from her grandmother and started selling small amigurumi to neighbours in 2024. The orders kept coming, and so did the same question with every delivery — can you teach me how to make this?

So now we do both. We still crochet every piece by hand, one at a time, and we teach the stitches behind them on video in six languages, or live with your hands in front of us.
